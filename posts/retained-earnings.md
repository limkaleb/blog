Next, let's look at **retained earnings**. ACES' total equity is around Rp 5.9 T as of 2022, and only 171 M + 440 M = Rp 611 M of that is actual capital. That means (5.9 T - 611 M) = Rp 5.3 T, or **90%**, comes from profit the company has collected over the years. And this doesn't even count dividends. If we added those back, the number would be much higher.

![modal-aces](../equity-aces.jpg)

Every Rp 1 of capital has become Rp 9.6 (dividends excluded). Put another way, their capital has grown 965% from the initial amount.

![laba-aces](../profit-aces.jpg)

ACES' profit for full-year 2022 was Rp 705 M, which is already higher than their initial capital of Rp 611 M.
