title: 'Austrian vs Keynesian Economics'
date: '2024-03-02'
---

My quick notes comparing the two schools of thought.

## Austrian Economics
- **Free markets** are efficient.
- Companies should be allowed to fail. That clears out the least efficient businesses and moves resources to the most efficient ones.
- Inflation is a bad thing because it destroys savings and devalues currency.
- Supports **sound money**: a currency backed by gold or other hard assets.
- Book: "Principles of Economics" by Carl Menger.
- Key figures: Carl Menger, Ludwig von Mises and Friedrich Hayek.


## Keynesian Economics
- **Free markets** are inefficient and volatile.
- Government should step in with policies to stabilize the economy and soften recessions.
- Supports fiat currency, which central banks can adjust through the money supply to keep the economy stable.
- Book: "The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money" by John Maynard Keynes.
- Key figure: John Maynard Keynes.
