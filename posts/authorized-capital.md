---
title: 'First Thing is Authorized Capital'
date: '2023-07-30'
---

This is my key takeaway from **"Cara Mudah Memahami Laporan Keuangan"**, a book by one of Indonesia's most senior investors, **Joeliardi Sunendar**. I'm writing this mostly to remind myself in the future.

Before putting money into a company, it's essential to read its financial report first. One metric I like to start with is **Equity**. Within it, the first thing to check is **Authorized capital** / modal dasar. Just go to the Equity section and start from the very top.

![modal-dasar](../equity-sido.jpg)

This shows that SIDO issued and fully paid 30M shares at Rp 50 each, so the company received a total of Rp 1.5 T.
A company can also do an IPO or issue new shares to the public (rights issue), or to certain parties only. Either way, if the new shares are sold at a higher price, the difference goes to **Additional paid-in capital** / tambahan modal disetor.

For example:
- The company issues 50 M new shares and sells them at Rp 150 each (remember the initial price is Rp 100), so the total is Rp 150 x 50 M = 7.5 T.
- Rp 100 x 50 M = 5 T goes to **issued and fully paid - share capital** / modal ditempatkan dan disetor penuh.
- The other 2.5 T goes to **additional paid-in capital** / tambahan modal disetor.

So in this case, as of Q2 2023, SIDO's initial equity (modal awal) is 1.5 T + 652 M = Rp 2.15 T.
